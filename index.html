        About

        Most of what I've built started as somebody's real problem.

        A restaurant's floor. A journal's submission spec. A wheat farmer's harvest window. A rescue crew's drone feed. I like the parts of a system other people skip: schema design, ingestion throughput, and the difference between a model that scores well and a model you can defend to a stakeholder.

        When a project wins something I try not to let it end there. WaiterDock is the clearest case: we won the sponsor's challenge at TAMUhack, and instead of posting about it I asked whether they'd like it running in their restaurant. They did.
